# CSC Scholarship Preparation

This folder contains documents, research notes, and preparation materials related to my application for the Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) for a Master's degree in China.

## 🎯 Purpose

To organize my research proposals, professor outreach drafts, and study plans as I work toward studying in China.

## 📂 Contents

- **Research Proposals:** Drafts and final versions of research proposals tailored to different professors.
- **Professor Outreaches:** Templates and drafts used to contact potential supervisors.
- **Study Plans:** Timelines and checklists for HSK and IELTS exams.
